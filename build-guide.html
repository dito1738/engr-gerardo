Open-Source Build Guide

Accessible Gaming Mouse & Foot Pedal Setup

Final assembly showing the handpiece interface and footmount pedals. Click to enlarge.

Required 3D Print Files

Download Handpiece STL Download Footmount STL

*The mouse bottom is an open-source Bambu file. Link to Bambu original here.

Recommended Slicer Orientation

Global Settings: Prusa MK4 (0.4mm nozzle) • 0.20mm SPEED • PLA • 15% Infill
Handpiece (v3)
  • Time: 2h 49m
  • Material: 104.46g (35.03m)
Footmount (v3)
  • Time: 3h 4m
  • Material: 140.50g (47.11m)
Durability Specs
Handpiece:

500N Top-down (no break), 400N side wall (break), survived stair drop test.

Mousepiece:

50N battery section limit. Housing is durable but not intended for heavy force.

Bill of Materials
  • 1x Bambu Mouse Kit
  • 2x PCB Fiber Boards
  • 2x Microswitches
  • 22 AWG Wire
  • Soldering Kit
  • PLA/PETG Filament
Documentation Note

This build guide is currently text-based. Step-by-step photographic assembly instructions are currently in production and will be added soon. Please read each step carefully.

Step-by-Step Assembly

1
Print Components

Print the custom handpiece and modified mouse bottom using PLA or PETG.

2
Measure & Print Footmount

Measure your PCB fiber boards and adjust the footmount CAD cutouts (3mm tolerance) before printing.

3
Assemble Motherboard

Follow standard Bambu Wireless Kit instructions for the base motherboard setup.

4
Desolder Switches

Carefully remove the built-in left and right click microswitches from the Bambu motherboard.

5
Attach Extension Wires

Solder extension wires to the contact pads where the original buttons were located.

6
Prepare Pedal Boards

Solder your new microswitch buttons onto the two PCB fiber boards.

7
Route & Solder

Route wires through the handpiece/footmount holes and solder them to the pedal boards.

8
Test Functionality

Verify the mouse registers clicks when the pedals are pressed before final gluing.

9
Final Glue-Up

Secure the boards into the footmount and glue the handpiece to the mouse bottom housing.

Complete!

You are now ready to play FPS games with your new setup.
