Open-Source Build Guide

Accessible Gaming Mouse & Foot Pedal Setup

Required 3D Print Files

Download Handpiece STL Download Footmount STL

*The mouse bottom is an open-source Bambu file. Link to Bambu original here.

Durability Specs
Handpiece:
  • Top-Down Force: 500N (Max) - No break
  • Top Side Wall: 400N - Break
  • Bottom Side Wall: 240N - Break
  • Drop Test: Survived stairs
Mousepiece:
  • Top-Down: 50N on battery section
  • Body: Hard to break manually
  • Note: Unlikely to take direct force
Bill of Materials
  • 1x Bambu Wireless Mouse Kit
  • 2x PCB Fiber Boards (Identical dims)
  • 2x Microswitch Buttons
  • Electrical Wire
  • Soldering Kit
  • PLA or PETG Filament
  • Adhesive (Glue/Epoxy)

Step-by-Step Assembly

1
Print the Components

3D print the custom handpiece and the modified mouse bottom housing using PLA or PETG.

2
Measure & Print Footmount

Measure the dimensions of your PCB fiber boards. Update the CAD for the footmount to ensure cutouts match your boards exactly (leave a minimum tolerance of ~3mm). Print the footmount.

3
Assemble Motherboard

Assemble the core motherboard of the Bambu wireless kit according to their standard instructions.

4
Desolder Standard Switches

Desolder and remove the left and right click buttons from the Bambu motherboard.

5
Attach Extension Wires

Solder two wires into each side of the motherboard where the original buttons were removed.

6
Prepare Foot Pedal Boards

Solder one replacement microswitch button onto each of your two PCB fiber boards.

7
Route & Complete Circuit

Run the wires through the routing holes in the handpiece and down into the footmount. Solder the wires to the buttons on the fiber boards to close the circuit.

8
Test the Circuit

Turn on the mouse and verify that the left and right click buttons on your foot pedals work correctly on a computer.

9
Final Assembly

Glue the fiber boards into the footmount cutouts. Glue the motherboard to the mouse bottom. Finally, glue the handpiece to the mouse bottom where it clips in.

Play!

Compete in FPS games with your new hands-free clicking setup.
